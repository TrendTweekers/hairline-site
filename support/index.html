Support

Need help? Email trendtweekers00@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Is the Hairline Check an AI scan?

No. It is an honest self-assessment: you answer four questions and we map them to a Norwood-stage estimate. It is a guide, not a fake AI score and not a diagnosis. Only a dermatologist can assess hair loss.

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded. If you delete the app, your photos are deleted too.

Why does it ask for camera access?

So you can take progress photos. The photos stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

How do I get the best comparison?

Use the same light, angle, and distance each time. The ghost overlay lines your new photo up with the last one so the comparison is honest.

Purchases and restore

Optional purchases are handled by Apple. You can manage or cancel them in your Apple ID settings, and restore them from within the app.

Hairline is a tracking tool, not medical advice or a diagnosis. Talk to a dermatologist about treatment decisions.
